How payment cycles work

The 38-week season is split into 7 payment cycles. Players pay upfront per cycle to cover their ₦2,000/week stake — ₦10,000 for cycles 1–6, and ₦16,000 for the final cycle (GW31–38, 8 weeks). Tap Manage on any cycle to mark who has paid.
